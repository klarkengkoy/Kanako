Kanako helps you read Japanese by adding reading guides to text. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and how you can delete it.

Your images stay on your device

Kanako reads screenshots, camera images and photos on your device. It does not send those images or the text it reads to us or to an online reading service.

Screenshots of other apps are held in memory only while they are needed. Kanako does not save or upload them. Camera captures are not saved automatically.

When you choose a photo, Kanako can read that photo. If it comes from a cloud photo service, that service may download it to your device. Its own privacy policy applies.

The gallery can show images previously saved by Kanako, along with their names and save dates. Saved files may remain after you uninstall the app. If you choose to share an image, it goes to the app or person you select.

You control screen and camera access

The floating reader uses Android’s Accessibility Service to take a screenshot and place readings over another app. You must agree to this and turn on access in Android Settings. Screen reading needs Android 11 or later.

Kanako uses information about the current app, window, scrolling and keyboard to keep readings in the right place and avoid reading sensitive screens. It does not collect what you type through Android’s accessibility controls.

Screen capture is blocked when the screen is locked or when a keyboard or password field is detected. These checks cannot identify every private detail that might appear in an image.

Automatic reading is off by default and needs your separate agreement. You can stop the reader from its controls, Home or its notification, and turn off its permissions in Android Settings.

Camera permission is used to read through your camera. Choosing a photo does not require camera permission. Notification permission lets Kanako show its reader notification.

Signing in with Google

Kanako uses Google and Firebase Authentication, Google’s sign-in service. They handle the information needed to sign you in, such as your email address, account ID and sign-in details. Google may also provide basic profile information. Kanako uses your account ID and email to identify your account and show who is signed in. We do not ask for your Google password.

For security and to run sign-in, Firebase also handles information such as your network address and details about the app and sign-in software. Read Firebase’s data information for more detail.

In supported builds, Reviewer sign-in lets approved reviewers sign in with a dedicated Firebase account. The account service checks that they are approved before allowing reading. This access does not start a trial, subscription or Google Play purchase.

Information Google’s reading software uses

Kanako uses Google’s ML Kit software to recognize text on your device. The software also sends Google technical information to help keep it working and improve it. This can include your phone model, Android and app versions, device or installation IDs, processing speed, settings, model downloads and errors. This is separate from sending your images or the text they contain.

Read ML Kit’s data information, Google’s Privacy Policy and Firebase’s privacy information for more detail. Google may process information outside your country. Kanako does not sell your personal information or use your reading content for advertising.

Settings and saved information

Your reading settings, permission choices and tutorial progress are stored on your device. Android may copy app data during backup or transfer, depending on your system settings.

Uninstalling Kanako does not delete your online account or separately saved images. You can delete saved images using your device’s photo or file app.

Your Google sign-in information remains with Firebase while you keep your Kanako account. Google handles its own sign-in and technical records under its policies. No storage or connection can be guaranteed completely secure.

Contacting us and deleting your account

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you send so we can help you. Please do not send passwords, sign-in codes or unnecessary private images. We delete support conversations and unneeded attachments within 90 days after the issue is resolved.

You can ask us to delete your Kanako sign-in account by email. Deleting it does not delete your Google account or your saved image files.

You can also email smyddevstudio@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Kanako account”, preferably from the email you used to sign in. We check that the account belongs to you before deleting it. If you cannot use that email address, contact us for help.

We normally complete emailed deletion requests in 7–14 days, and no later than 14 days after receiving them. You do not need to reinstall Kanako to ask. Dedicated reviewer accounts are managed by us; reviewers can sign out to end their session.

Your choices and questions

You can turn off permissions or automatic reading, sign out, and contact us to ask about, correct or delete your personal information. Your privacy rights depend on where you live.

We will update this policy if our practices change. For privacy questions, contact Samoyed Dev Studio at smyddevstudio@gmail.com.
